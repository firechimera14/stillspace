Support

Need help? Email our support team and include your Windows or macOS version.

Add the browser extension (required for website blocking)

Download it from the Download page, unzip it, then:
1. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
2. Click Load unpacked and pick the extension folder from your StillSpace download.
3. In StillSpace, open Blocklist: it should say the extension is connected.

The window is blank

Close StillSpace from the tray icon, then reopen it. If it persists, start it from PowerShell with $env:FOCUS_NO_GPU=1 first.

Websites are not blocked

Run StillSpace as administrator once, and make sure the extension is connected.
